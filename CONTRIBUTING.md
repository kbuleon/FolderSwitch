# Contributing to FolderSwitch

FolderSwitch is a personal project, written and maintained by one author.

- **Bug reports and suggestions are welcome.** Open an issue with the « Bug report » or « Suggestion » form: a bug
  report asks what you did, what you expected and what happened, with the version (shown in the title bar and the
  About window) and your Windows version.
- **Security vulnerabilities are reported privately**, never in an issue: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Code contributions are not accepted**: the repository does not take pull requests. This keeps a single copyright
  holder for the whole code, who remains free to choose its license.

You may still fork FolderSwitch under the terms of its license, the GPL-3.0-or-later with the additional terms of the
README's « License » section: among them, a modified version must not be called « FolderSwitch » nor use its icon.
