# Contributing to FolderSwitch

FolderSwitch is a personal project, written and maintained by one author.

- **Bug reports and suggestions are welcome.** Open an issue: say what you did, what you expected and what happened,
  with the version (shown in the title bar and the About window) and your Windows version.
- **Code contributions are not accepted.** A pull request will be closed, with thanks, and a link to this page. This
  keeps a single copyright holder for the whole code, who remains free to choose its license.

You may still fork FolderSwitch under the terms of its license, the GPL-3.0-or-later with the additional terms of the
README's « License » section: among them, a modified version must not be called « FolderSwitch » nor use its icon.
